The steps

Fill. Push. Fizz.

Every step takes seconds. Here’s exactly what to do, and why it works.

  1. 01

    Step 01

    Fill it with cold water

    Fill the bottle to the line on the side, nothing more. Then you’re ready for the next step.

    • Cold water
    • Up to the fill line
    • 20 oz / 590 ml

    Why it matters Cold water carbonates best: it holds CO₂ longer and gives you a cleaner, crisper bubble.

  2. 02

    Step 02

    Lock in a CO₂ canister

    Push a Fizz canister into the base firmly until you hear it click, then give the cartridge a quarter turn to seal.

    • Push until it clicks
    • Quarter turn to seal
    • 1 canister ≈ 12 L

    Why it matters That quarter turn is what builds the pressure for perfect fizz.

  3. 03

    Step 03

    Press, hold, fizz

    Press the lid down and hold for a few seconds. Still water turns sparkling in seconds. Release when you hear the hiss, and enjoy.

    • Press & hold the lid
    • Listen for the hiss
    • Sparkling in seconds

    Why it matters The push-button lid is the whole machine. No dials, no settings.

  4. 04

    Step 04

    Add flavor (optional)

    Want more than plain sparkling? Drop a flavor pack into the bottle, shake, press and drink. Mixed Berry, Cherry Limeade or Orange Tangerine. Swap anytime.

    • 3 flavors
    • Made with natural flavors
    • Swap anytime

    Why it matters One bottle, any flavor: a different pour every time, with the same fizz.

The science, simply

How the fizz happens

Sparkling water is just water holding dissolved CO₂. Fizz puts that process in your hand.

  1. The canister

    A CO₂ canister locks into the base and seals with a quarter turn.

  2. The press

    Pressing the lid releases CO₂ into your water and builds pressure inside.

  3. The bubbles

    Under pressure the water takes on CO₂. Cold water holds onto it longer.

  4. The sip

    Release at the hiss and drink, at the gym, the beach, or in the car.

Meet the Fizz bottle

The Fizz Bottle

The bottle, reinvented.

A new portable carbonator built around a clear 20 oz Tritan body, a push-button lid, and a hidden CO₂ base. No machine. No cords. Just fill, push, and drink.

The canister

Sparkling water on the go.

The Fizz Canister is small enough that a pack of six slips into a purse or bag. Each one carbonates up to 12 liters — about 33 cans of sparkle — then lives in the bottle base until you need a refill.

Fill

Cold water holds CO₂ best, and gives you a cleaner, crisper bubble. Fill to the line, nothing more, and you're set for the next step.

Push

Lock a CO₂ Charge into the base until it clicks, then give it a quarter turn. That seal is what builds pressure for a proper fizz.

Fizz

Press the lid and hold for a few seconds. Still water turns sparkling. Listen for the soft hiss, then drink.

FILL

Cold water carbonates best — it holds CO₂ longer and gives you a cleaner, crisper bubble. Fill to the line on the bottle, nothing more, and you're ready for the next step.

PUSH

Lock a CO₂ canister into the base with a firm push until you hear it click. Give the cartridge a quarter turn to seal — that's what builds the pressure for perfect fizz.

FIZZ

Press the lid down and hold for a few seconds. Still water turns sparkling in seconds — release when you hear the hiss and enjoy.

About Fizz

Sparkling water,
minus the cans.

Fizz is a portable sparkling water bottle. Refill the bottle, refill the CO₂ and make your own sparkle anywhere.

Why Fizz

Sparkle shouldn’t be stuck on the counter.

Sparkling water usually means a countertop machine or a fridge full of cans. Fizz puts the carbonation in the bottle, so plain water turns sparkling wherever you are — the gym, the beach, the car, your desk.

It’s a simple system: a refillable bottle, a refillable CO₂ charge and drop-in flavor packs. Fill it, charge it, press it. That’s the whole machine.

Every bottle you fizz yourself is one you didn’t have to buy, chill, carry home and recycle.

One charge, a lot of cans

1 Fizz Charge ≈ 33 cans

Each charge carbonates up to 12 liters of water — about the same as 33 twelve-ounce cans of sparkling water.

Sustainability

Refill. Re-fizz. Repeat.

  • One bottle, on repeat

    A durable, BPA-free Tritan bottle you refill again and again — instead of buying sparkling water in single-use containers.

  • Refillable CO₂

    Fizz Charge canisters are refillable. The CO₂ is what gets used up, not the canister.

  • Less to haul home

    A 6-pack of charges carbonates up to 72 liters. That’s a lot fewer cases of cans to carry.

  • Natural flavors

    Fizz flavor packs are made with natural flavors. Add one when you want it, skip it when you don’t.

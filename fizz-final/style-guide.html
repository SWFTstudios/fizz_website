01 · Color

Fizz Deep

A dark, cool palette built around deep water. Surfaces stay quiet so the lifestyle photography and the ice-blue actions carry the color. Click any swatch to copy its hex.

Surfaces

Darkest to lightest. Pages sit on Deep; cards sit on Surface; hovers and highlights lift to Raised.

  • Abyss--fizz-abyss · #070E12Intro, menu panel, deepest overlays
  • Deep--fizz-deep · #0C171DPage background (default)
  • Surface--fizz-surface · #12222ACards, panels, inputs
  • Raised--fizz-raised · #193039Hover, selected rows, gradients
  • Line--fizz-line · rgba(214,238,248,.12)Borders and dividers

Text

Mist for headings and key copy, Fog for body, Haze for hints and captions at 16px+.

  • 16.6:1Mist--fizz-mist · #EEF6F9Headings, primary text
  • 9.0:1Fog--fizz-fog · #A7BAC3Body and secondary text
  • 4.8:1Haze--fizz-haze · #6F8792Captions, inactive tabs (large text)

Accents

One accent per moment. Ice is for the action you want people to take; Spark is for things that move or are live.

Pairing with photography

Our photos are bright, outdoor and blue-skied. The dark palette frames them like a gallery wall: let images run edge to edge, keep type on Deep or on a Deep photo fade, and let Ice pick up the sky.

07 · Imagery

Real people, real places, real bubbles

Bright outdoor photography with the bottle in hand. Let it run big; frames crop to cover, never stretch.

  • Do use object-fit: cover in a fixed-ratio frame, with responsive srcset sizes.
  • Do put text over photos only on a Deep photo fade.
  • Don’t crop through the bottle or the hand holding it.
  • Do write alt text that says what’s happening (“Hand pressing the push-button lid”).

08 · Icons & logo

Simple lines, confident mark

Icons are 24px grid line icons with a 1.7 stroke and round caps, drawn in currentColor. The FIZZ wordmark is white on dark.

Icon set

Wordmark

  • Clear space: at least the width of the I on every side.
  • White on Deep/Abyss or over a Deep photo fade; never on busy imagery.
  • Don’t recolor, outline, stretch or add effects.

09 · Voice

Bright, brief, a little bubbly

We sound like a friend who’s really into sparkling water: clear first, playful second.

Do

  • “Fill it, load a canister, press it.”
  • “Release when you hear the hiss.”
  • “Sparkling water, minus the cans.”
  • Lead with the benefit, then the detail.

Don’t

  • “Utilize the carbonation apparatus.”
  • Over-promise (“zero waste”, “medical-grade hydration”).
  • Stack exclamation marks!!!
  • Use CO2 without the subscript.

Product names

  • Fizz Bottle: the bottle (Stone, Onyx, Pacific)
  • Fizz Canister: CO₂ refill 6-pack
  • Fizz Flavors: flavor packs
  • Starter Kit: bottle + canister + 3 flavors

10 · Accessibility

Built for everyone

Measured, not guessed: contrast ratios below are WCAG 2.1 values against Deep.

Contrast on Deep

Mist16.6:1Any text
Ice13.6:1Any text
Spark11.0:1Any text
Fog9.0:1Any text
Citrus8.6:1Any text
Haze4.8:1Text ≥ 16px
Berry4.6:1Large text, swatches
Cobalt3.0:1Fills only

Always

  • Tap targets at least 44 × 44px.
  • Visible focus: 2px Spark/Ice outline, 3px offset.
  • Every icon-only button has an aria-label; option groups use role="radiogroup".
  • Status changes (added to cart, errors) go to an aria-live region.
  • prefers-reduced-motion removes the intro, transitions and autoplay.
  • Dialogs trap focus and close on Escape.

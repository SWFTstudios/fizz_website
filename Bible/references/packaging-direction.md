## How it works (short)

1. Insert the slim CO₂ cartridge (multiple refills).
2. Pull up the TAB to carbonate (up to 8 times).
3. Twist threaded lid; sip from spout.
4. Optional flavor packet after carbonating.

## Kit

- 1× Fizz bottle
- 1× CO₂ cartridge
- 2× flavor packets

## Features

- Cup-holder friendly
- Locking leak-proof lid
- 1 bottle ≈ 15 cans of sparkling water (sustainability angle)
